# Review cards show the word and ask for its meaning

## Decision

A card's front is the word. Its back is the part of speech, the definition, the phrase from Alex's prompt that the word replaces, and the prompt restated with the word.

## Why

Alex chose classic Anki recall of meaning over producing the word from his own phrasing.

## Rejected

- **Recall the word from your own wording (a cloze of the rewrite).** Trains producing the word while writing, but Alex preferred recalling the meaning.
